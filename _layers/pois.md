---
layer: pois
title: POIs
summary: "Points of interest: businesses, tourist attractions, and other places people go"
---

A feature lands in POIs if it carries at least one of twelve top-level tags:
`amenity`, `attraction`, `club`, `craft`, `education`, `healthcare`, `historic`
(excluding `historic=yes` and `historic=no`), `leisure`, `office`, `playground`,
`shop` or `tourism`.
